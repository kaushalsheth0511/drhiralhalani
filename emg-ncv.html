Diagnostic service

EMG & nerve conduction studies

Performed and reported by Dr. Hiral herself, in the same sitting.

NCV / NCS

Nerve conduction study — testing the wiring

Small electrical pulses measure how fast and how strongly a nerve conducts.

EMG

Needle EMG — listening to the muscle

A fine needle electrode records the electrical activity of the muscle.

RNS

Repetitive nerve stimulation

Supports the diagnosis of myasthenia gravis.

Also available

EEG and other neurophysiology

EEG for seizures and unexplained loss of consciousness.

On the day

What actually happens, step by step.

01

Examination first

Your history is taken and you are examined first.

02

Nerve conduction

Surface electrodes and brief pulses; limbs are warmed first.

03

Needle EMG, if needed

A few muscles are sampled with a fine needle where needed.

04

Explained before you leave

Findings are discussed with you in the same visit.

A study usually takes 30 to 60 minutes.

Before you come

How to prepare

  • Eat normally and take your usual medicines.
  • Do not apply oil, lotion or cream on the arms and legs.
  • Wear loose clothing.
  • Bring all previous reports.
  • Bring a list of your medicines.
  • Cold limbs are warmed before testing.

Does it hurt?

Please mention before the test
